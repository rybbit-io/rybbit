Mixpanel pricing comes in three plans. **Free** costs $0 for up to 1 million events a month. **Growth** also starts at $0, because the first 1 million events each month are free; above that, Mixpanel's own calculator prices it from $140 a month for 1.5 million events to $2,289 a month for 20 million on monthly billing, or $120 to $1,635 a month billed annually. **Enterprise** is quoted by sales, and the user-based (MTU) version starts at $20,000 a year. Seats are unlimited on every plan, and add-ons such as Group Analytics and Data Pipelines have no public price.

## How Mixpanel pricing works

By default, Mixpanel bills on events, not on users or seats. An event is one tracked action (a page view, a sign-up, a purchase), and the [pricing docs](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/pricing) count them "across all projects in your organization". A handful of system events are excluded: `$identify`, `$create_alias`, `$merge`, `$opt_in`, session recording checkpoints, and user profile creation or updates.

Three rules decide how large that count gets:

- **Autocapture counts.** Mixpanel's [Autocapture](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/tracking-methods/autocapture) records page views, scrolls, form interactions, element clicks, dead clicks and rage clicks by default, and the [heatmap docs](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/session-replay/heatmaps) state that autocaptured clicks are billable. Page views and heatmap clicks recorded while a session replay is running are exempt.
- **Imports count when they arrive.** Since September 1, 2025, Mixpanel bills by ingestion time (some older accounts still bill by event timestamp), so backfilling last year's data counts toward this month's total.
- **Warehouse syncs count updates.** With Mirror-type warehouse syncs, every update or deletion of an existing event counts as one event.

Pricing by monthly tracked users (MTUs) still exists, but only as an Enterprise contract. Mixpanel's [MTU guide](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/pricing/legacy-mtu-billing) says MTU plans "start at $20,000 per year", can't be bought online, and are worth considering above 100k users or 5M monthly events. Anonymous visitors count as MTUs.

## The Mixpanel free plan: what's included and where it stops

The Free plan is "free forever" and needs no card. It includes unlimited seats, up to 1M events a month, 10k session replays, funnels and retention reports, 1k MEU for experiments, and up to 10 active feature flags.

The limits, from the [pricing docs](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/pricing) and the plan comparison table on the pricing page:

- **5 saved reports per user.** You can build cohorts but not save them.
- **Missing features.** No custom or borrowed properties, multi-touch attribution, Query API, warehouse connectors or alerts, and lookup tables are limited.
- **The 1M cap is hard.** Mixpanel keeps collecting data, but once you reach 1M events it blocks your reports until you upgrade or the month resets. There are no overage charges.
- **Replays expire after 30 days**, and once the 10k allowance is used you can't view more replays until you upgrade.
- **Data history isn't unlimited.** Mixpanel's [free-plan page](https://mixpanel.com/free-plan/) advertises "unlimited data history", but its [data retention policy](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/privacy/gdpr-compliance) deletes events after 2 years for projects created since September 1, 2025. Older projects keep 5 years until their plan changes or moves to Free. Only Enterprise lists a customizable retention policy.

How to read the table:

- **Annual billing is about 29% cheaper** from 2M events up: at every step from 2M to 20M, the monthly price is about 1.4 times the annual one. Annual plans require a commitment above 1M events a month, and the calculator shows their allowance as a yearly total (120M events a year at the 10M tier).
- **The only published per-event rate is $0.00028**, which is what overage costs on the 1M plan ($0.28 per 1,000 events). Committing to a tier lowers the effective rate: on monthly billing the 10M tier works out to $1,176 ÷ 9M billable events, about $0.13 per 1,000.
- **The calculator prices events only.** Extra replays, extra experiment users and add-ons are billed separately.

What Growth adds over Free: unlimited saved reports and cohorts, custom and borrowed properties, multi-touch attribution, up to 5 alerts per project, Query API access, data warehouse connectors and, since February 24, 2026, SSO, Data Views and sensitive data classification. The pricing docs describe the typical Growth customer as a team with "fewer than 50 employees".

## What happens when you go over your plan

On Growth, Mixpanel keeps collecting. Overage is billed at the start of the next month at your "À La Carte" rate, which is $0.00028 per event on the 1M plan and lower on bigger tiers. If you have add-ons, the [pricing docs](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/pricing) add 20% of the overage for Data Pipelines and 40% for Group Analytics.

There is no spending cap; the docs say "it's not possible to set a billing limit at this moment". Owners and billing admins get emails at 85%, 100%, 110%, 120%, 200%, 300% and 400% of plan volume.

The other meters work differently. Experiment users beyond your allowance are billed at an overage rate Mixpanel doesn't publish. Replays beyond the included allowance can't be viewed unless you've bought the replay add-on; with it, replays beyond your purchased amount are billed at your per-unit rate. And if you decide to drop back to Free, the downgrade takes effect at the next billing date, not immediately.

## Mixpanel Enterprise pricing

Enterprise has no list price; the pricing page says "Let's chat". It raises the event ceiling to 1 trillion a month and adds what Growth lacks: anomaly detection, root cause analysis, signal correlation, cross-product analytics, data standards, event owners and approval, data quality monitoring, verified data and cohorts, HIPAA compliance tools, first-party tracking domains, a customizable data retention policy, 24/7 support and a dedicated account manager. Metric Trees and the Slack and SLA support options are Enterprise add-ons. The MTU-based Enterprise plan is the only one with a published floor: $20,000 a year.

## Mixpanel startup program

The [startup program](https://docs.mixpanel.com/docs/pricing/startup-program) is open to companies incorporated less than five years ago that have raised no more than $8 million, haven't redeemed another Mixpanel offer, and have never been on a paid plan. They get the first year free on a Startup Plan with 1 billion events a year, 500,000 session replays, Experiments with 1.2 million MEU a year, 50 feature flags, and the Data Pipelines, Group Analytics, Warehouse Connectors and Session Replay add-ons. You must start sending data within 90 days, and there's no second free year. The FAQ on the pricing page puts the replay allowance at 1 million, so confirm which figure applies when you apply.

## Three worked examples

### A new app under 1M events

4,000 monthly active users × 120 events each = 480,000 events a month.

- Free plan: **$0**, with room to double before the 1M cap.
- If the team needs saved cohorts, alerts or more than 5 saved reports per person, Growth is also **$0** at this volume. It needs a card, and any month over 1M is billed at $0.00028 per extra event.

### A consumer app at 4M events

25,000 monthly active users × 160 events each = 4,000,000 events a month.

- Growth, billed monthly: $504 × 12 = **$6,048 a year**.
- Growth, billed annually: $360 × 12 = **$4,320 a year**.
- Annual billing saves $1,728 a year, which matches the saving the calculator shows. Recording more than 20k sessions a month would add the replay add-on on top.

### A B2B SaaS that never resized its plan

The team signed up for the 1M-event Growth plan ($0) with Group Analytics and now sends 3M events a month.

- Event overage: 2,000,000 × $0.00028 = $560.
- Group Analytics surcharge: 40% × $560 = $224.
- Total: **$784 a month**, plus the Group Analytics fee itself.
- The 3M tier lists at **$378 a month** billed monthly ($270 billed annually), $182 less than the event overage alone. Mixpanel doesn't publish how the Group Analytics fee changes between tiers, so ask for both totals before switching.

### Event counts aren't interchangeable

All three tools below bill per event, but they don't count the same things:

- **Mixpanel** counts every tracked event, including everything Autocapture records (page views, scrolls, clicks, form interactions), across all projects.
- **[PostHog](/compare/posthog)** bills product analytics "on events captured – pageviews, clicks, custom events, and everything autocapture picks up", per its [pricing page](https://posthog.com/product-analytics/pricing). Our [PostHog pricing breakdown](/blog/posthog-pricing) covers its other products.
- **Rybbit** counts pageviews and outbound-link clicks (both tracked by default) plus the [custom events](/features/custom-events) you send. [Web Vitals](/features/web-vitals), button clicks, copies and form interactions count only if you switch them on.

The same traffic therefore produces different totals. A site that tracks only page views sends about one event per view to each tool; turn on autocapture in Mixpanel or PostHog and every captured click adds to the count. Estimate your volume per tool before comparing prices.

### Who should stay on Mixpanel

- **You run experiments and feature flags from your analytics tool.** Since August 2026 both are included on Free and Growth, with CUPED, sequential testing and sample-ratio-mismatch monitoring.

## FAQ

**How much does Mixpanel cost?**
Mixpanel Free costs $0 up to 1M events a month. Growth is $0 for the first 1M events, then $140 to $2,289 a month on monthly billing (1.5M to 20M events), or $120 to $1,635 a month billed annually. Enterprise is quoted by sales, with MTU-based contracts from $20,000 a year. Prices are as of September 2026.

**Is Mixpanel free?**
Yes. The Free plan is free forever with no card: 1M events a month, 10k session replays, unlimited seats and 5 saved reports per user. Growth also costs $0 up to 1M events but requires a card.

**What are Mixpanel's pricing plans?**
Free, Growth and Enterprise, all billed on monthly events. Growth and Enterprise can add Group Analytics, Data Pipelines, extra session replays and extra experiment volume; Enterprise also offers Metric Trees and an MTU-based contract.

**Is there a Mixpanel pricing calculator?**
Yes, on [mixpanel.com/pricing](https://mixpanel.com/pricing/). It covers Growth event volumes from 1M to 20M a month, with monthly and annual prices; the full table is above. It doesn't price add-ons or extra replays.

**How much does Mixpanel charge per event?**
The published rate is $0.00028 per event above 1M on the 1M Growth plan, or $0.28 per 1,000 events. Larger tiers are cheaper per event: the 10M tier works out to about $0.13 per 1,000 billable events on monthly billing.

**Does Mixpanel charge per user?**
Not by default. Seats are unlimited on every plan, and standard pricing is per event. Pricing by monthly tracked users is available only on Enterprise contracts, from $20,000 a year.

**Is Mixpanel session replay free?**
Up to a limit: 10k replays a month on Free and 20k on Growth and Enterprise, kept for 30 days. Growth customers can buy up to 500k a month, with the price shown in the in-app plan builder.

**What changed in Mixpanel pricing in 2025 and 2026?**
In February 2025, Growth started including 1M free events a month (it previously started at $24 a month). In September 2025, billing moved to ingestion time and default retention dropped to 2 years. SSO came to Growth in February 2026, and Experiments and Feature Flags were added to Free and Growth in August 2026.
