Open organization settings by clicking **Organization** in the settings sidebar or going to [app.rybbit.io/settings/organization](https://app.rybbit.io/settings/organization). Only admins and owners see the **Organization** and **Teams** items.

![organization settings](/docs/organization-settings.png)

The page has four sections: **General**, **People**, **Organization API keys** and **Danger zone**.

## General

- **Name** - Shown in the navigation and on invitations. Admins and owners can change it.
- **Slug** - Set when the organization is created. It can't be changed.
- **Created** - When the organization was created.

## People

The **People** table lists everyone in the organization, with a search box to find someone by name or email. It has these columns:

- **Person** - Name and email
- **Role** - Owner, Admin, Editor, Member, or Viewer
- **Site access** - **All sites**, or how many of the organization's sites they can reach, with the teams and direct grants that give them access
- **Joined** - When they joined

Pending invitations appear in the same table, marked **Pending**, with who sent them and when they expire. Use **Resend** to send an invitation again or **Cancel** to revoke it.

Click a member's edit button to change their role or site access, or to remove them from the organization with **Remove Member**. Only system admins (accounts with the instance-wide admin role) can also change a member's name there.

### Member Roles

Each role can do everything the roles below it can.

| Role | What it adds |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Billing and plans, deleting the organization, making other owners |
| **Admin** | Inviting and managing members and teams, organization API keys, creating, deleting and [moving](/docs/site-settings#move-to-organization) sites |
| **Editor** | Site configuration: tracking settings, exclusions, public and private links, feature flags, experiments, data imports, Google Search Console, deleting session replays and tracked users, and editing anyone's segments and annotations |
| **Member** | Building reports: goals, funnels, dashboards, and their own segments and annotations |
| **Viewer** | Viewing analytics, sessions, replays and reports, without changing anything |

Admins and owners always reach every site in the organization. Editors, members and viewers reach every site too, unless you [restrict them to specific sites](/docs/inviting-users#restricting-site-access) or the site belongs to a [team](/docs/teams) they aren't on.

A member's role can also be raised on individual sites: when you restrict someone to specific sites, or add a site to a team, you can give them a higher role on just those sites. For example, a client can be a **Viewer** in your organization and an **Editor** on their own site. Nobody can give a role above their own, and only owners can make owners.

### Inviting Members

Owners and admins can invite new members (Rybbit Cloud) or create user accounts (self-hosted) with the button next to the search box. See the [Inviting Users](/docs/inviting-users) guide for detailed instructions. A person can belong to several organizations.

### Teams

You can organize sites into teams to control which members have access to which sites. See the [Teams](/docs/teams) guide for details.

## Organization API keys

Organization API keys belong to the organization rather than a person, so they keep working when people leave. Only admins and owners can see or create them. A full-access organization key can reach all of the organization's sites; turn on **Restrict permissions** to limit it to specific resources. On Rybbit Cloud they share the organization's daily request budget and need a Standard or Pro plan.

Creating and managing them works the same way as [personal API keys](/docs/account-settings#personal-api-keys). For keys that act as you, with your own role, use personal keys instead.

## Danger zone

### Delete organization

Deleting an organization removes its members, teams and organization API keys. It does not delete sites: an organization that still has sites can't be deleted. Delete each site, or move it to another organization from its [site settings](/docs/site-settings), before you delete the organization. Only owners can delete an organization.

To confirm, click **Delete organization**, type the organization's name, then press and hold **Hold to delete organization**.

<Callout type="warn">
  This cannot be undone. If the organization has an active subscription, cancel it in [Billing](/docs/billing-settings) first.
</Callout>
