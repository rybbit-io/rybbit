Access organization settings by clicking on "Organization" or going to [app.rybbit.io/settings/organization](https://app.rybbit.io/settings/organization).

![organization settings](/docs/organization-settings.png)

## Organization

### Organization Name

Update your organization's display name. Owners and admins can change this setting.

### Delete Organization

Permanently delete the organization and all associated data including sites, analytics, reports, and member associations. Only owners can delete an organization.

<Callout type="warn">
  This action cannot be undone. You must type the organization name to confirm deletion. If you have an active subscription, you must cancel it before deleting the organization.
</Callout>

## Members

View and manage all members in your organization. The members table shows:
- **Name** - Member's display name
- **Email** - Member's email address
- **Role** - Owner, Admin, Editor, Member, or Viewer
- **Site Access** - Which sites the member can access
- **Joined** - When they joined the organization

### Member Roles

Each role can do everything the roles below it can.

| Role | What it adds |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Billing and plans, deleting the organization, making other owners |
| **Admin** | Inviting and managing members and teams, organization API keys, creating, deleting and [transferring](/docs/site-settings#transfer-site) sites |
| **Editor** | Site configuration: tracking settings, exclusions, public and private links, feature flags, experiments, data imports, Google Search Console, deleting session replays and tracked users, and editing anyone's segments and annotations |
| **Member** | Building reports: goals, funnels, dashboards, and their own segments and annotations |
| **Viewer** | Viewing analytics, sessions, replays and reports, without changing anything |

Admins and owners always reach every site in the organization. Editors, members and viewers reach every site too, unless you [restrict them to specific sites](/docs/inviting-users#restricting-site-access) or the site belongs to a [team](/docs/teams) they aren't on.

A member's role can also be raised on individual sites: when you restrict someone to specific sites, or add a site to a team, you can give them a higher role on just those sites. For example, a client can be a **Viewer** in your organization and an **Editor** on their own site. Nobody can give a role above their own, and only owners can make owners.

### Inviting Members

Owners and admins can invite new members or create user accounts. See the [Inviting Users](/docs/inviting-users) guide for detailed instructions. A person can belong to several organizations.

### Teams

You can organize sites into teams to control which members have access to which sites. See the [Teams](/docs/teams) guide for details.
