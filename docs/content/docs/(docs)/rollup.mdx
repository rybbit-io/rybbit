title: Rollup
description: See traffic across every site in your organization on one page
---

**Rollup** shows combined analytics for the sites in your organization, so you can compare them without opening each one. Open it from **Rollup** in the left navigation, below **Properties**, or search for it in the [command palette](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts).

## Choose which sites to include

- **Team**: pick a team to limit the page to that team's sites, **No Team** for sites without a team, or **All Teams**.
- **Tags**: pick one or more site tags to limit the page to sites with any of them. To tag a site, click the tag icon on its card on the home page.
- **Site chips**: each site in the current set has a colored chip. Click a chip to leave that site out or bring it back.

The date selector and the **Filter** button work like they do on a site's dashboard. Filters on the Rollup page are separate from the ones on your site dashboards.

## What it shows

- Totals for **Unique Users**, **Sessions**, **Pageviews**, **Pages per Session**, **Bounce Rate**, and **Session Duration**. Click one to chart it.
- A chart with one color per site. Users, sessions, and pageviews stack, since they add up across sites. Pages per session, bounce rate, and session duration show side by side instead.
- **Referrers** and **Channels**, plus UTM breakdowns.
- **Browsers**, **Devices**, **Operating Systems**, and screen dimensions.
- **Countries**, **Regions**, **Cities**, **Languages**, and **Timezones**.

You only see the sites you have access to.
