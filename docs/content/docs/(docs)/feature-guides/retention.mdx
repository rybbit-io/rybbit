Retention analysis shows how many users come back to your site over time, revealing whether users find lasting value. On Rybbit Cloud, retention needs a Standard plan or higher.

## Understanding Cohorts

A cohort is a group of new users who share a first-visit date. For example, all users who first visited on January 15th form one cohort. A user counts as new if they had no visit in the 90 days before the selected range. Someone returning after a longer gap counts as new again. Filters apply to each user's first visit.

Switch **Cohort period** between **Daily** and **Weekly**. Weekly cohorts start on Monday.

## Stats

The band at the top shows the average retention for the first periods (for example **Day 1 retention**), **Avg. cohort size**, and **Best cohort**: the cohort with the highest day 1 or week 1 retention among cohorts at least half the average size. With a comparison period on, each figure shows its change.

The chart shows average retention by period since first visit. Day 0 (or Week 0) is always 100%, so the curve starts at period 1.

## Reading the Retention Table

### Rows
Each row is a cohort, grouped by first visit date.

### Columns
- **Day/Week 0** - Starting point (always 100%)
- **Day/Week 1, 2, 3...** - Users who were active again in that period

### Numbers
Use **Show cells as** to switch between percentages (**%**) and **Users**. 40% in the "Day 7" column means 40% of that cohort was active again seven days after their first visit. Click a cell for details on who came back.

Cells for periods that haven't finished yet are marked as in progress, and their figures can still rise.

### Color Coding
Stronger color means higher retention. The legend above the table shows the scale.

Click **Export CSV** to download the table.

## Key Insights

- **Day 1 retention** - Did users find enough value to return the next day?
- **Day 7 retention** - Did users integrate your product into their routine?
- **Day 30 retention** - Have users formed a habit?

## Common Patterns

- **Cliff drop** - Sharp decline in first few days indicates onboarding issues
- **Steady decline** - Gradual drop suggests novelty wearing off
- **Plateau** - When retention levels off, you've found your core users

## Note on User ID Salting

If [User ID Salting](/docs/site-settings) is enabled, retention beyond 24 hours becomes less accurate since user identifiers reset daily.
