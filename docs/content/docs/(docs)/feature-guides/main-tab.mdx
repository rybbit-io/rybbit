The main dashboard is your home base in Rybbit Analytics, showing all key metrics at a glance.

## Top Metrics

- **Unique Users** - Different people who visited your site
- **Sessions** - Number of visits (30 minutes of inactivity starts a new session)
- **Pageviews** - Every page load from every visitor
- **Pages per Session** - Average pages viewed per visit
- **Bounce Rate** - Percentage of single-page sessions
- **Session Duration** - Average time spent per visit

Click a metric to plot it on the main chart.

## Time Controls

The date selector offers these presets: **Last 30 Minutes**, **Last 24 Hours**, **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days**, **Last 30 Days**, **This Week**, **This Month**, **This Year**, and **All Time**. Use **Custom range** to pick dates in the calendar.

To get any other window counted back from now, type it into the selector's search box, for example `90m`, `6h`, `60d`, or `2w`. Minutes and hours go up to a week; days and weeks go up to a year.

Each preset except This Week has a single-key shortcut when you're not typing in a field:

| Key | Range |
| --- | --- |
| `D` | Today |
| `E` | Yesterday |
| `H` | Last 24 Hours |
| `W` | Last 7 Days |
| `T` | Last 30 Days |
| `M` | This Month |
| `Y` | This Year |
| `A` | All Time |
| `R` | Last 30 Minutes |
| `C` | Custom range |

See [keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts) for the full list.

### Comparison

Compare the current range against another one. Choose **Previous period**, **Matching weekdays**, **Same period last year**, or **Custom range**, or **No comparison** to turn it off. The comparison shows on the chart and in the change figures on the top metrics.

## Filters

Combine filters for granular analysis:
- Device type, browser, OS
- Country, city
- Referrer, UTM parameters
- Custom event properties

A set of filters you use often can be saved as a [segment](/docs/segments) from the **Segments** tab of the Filter button, then applied to any report with one click.

## Main Chart

Shows the selected metric over time. Pick the time bucket (from **Min** up to **Year**) next to the chart. Drag across the chart to zoom into that range; double-click to zoom back out.

### Annotations

Click **Annotate**, or click a point on the chart, to pin a note to a moment, such as a launch, a deploy, or an outage. Pins appear along the chart; hover or click one to read it. See [Annotations](/docs/annotations).

## Referrers and Channels

The **Referrers** tab lists the sites that sent you traffic. The **Channels** tab groups every visit into one of 29 channels based on its referrer and UTM tags: Direct, Organic Search, Paid Search, Organic Social, Paid Social, AI, Paid AI, Email, SMS, Push, Referral, Affiliate, Display, Cross-Network, Organic Video, Paid Video, Organic Shopping, Paid Shopping, Audio, Paid Audio, Influencer, Paid Influencer, News, Content, Productivity, Event, Internal, Paid Unknown, and Unknown.

The **Source**, **Medium**, **Campaign**, **Term**, and **Content** tabs show UTM parameters.

Click any row to filter the entire dashboard to that traffic.

## Additional Breakdowns

- **Pages** - Most visited paths, plus tabs for **Titles**, **Entries**, **Exits**, and **Hostnames**
- **Devices** - **Browsers**, **Devices**, **Operating Systems**, and **Screen Dimensions**
- **Countries** - **Countries**, **Regions**, **Cities**, **Languages**, **Timezones**, and a **Map**
- **Events** - **Custom Events** and **Outbound** links, plus **Buttons**, **Forms**, and **Copies** when [autocapture](/docs/autocapture) is on
- **Weekly Trends** - A heatmap of the selected metric by weekday and hour
- **Search Console** (Rybbit Cloud) - **Keywords**, **Pages**, **Countries**, and **Devices** from Google Search Console, once you connect it with **Connect Google Search Console**

Click any row to filter the dashboard to it.
