Squarespace injects custom HTML into every page through its **Code Injection** panel, so no template edits are needed.

<Callout type="warn">
Code Injection is available on the **Business** and **Commerce** plans. It is not available on the Personal plan.
</Callout>

<Step>
### Add the snippet to Squarespace

1. From the Home Menu, open **Settings**.
2. Open **Website**, then **Code Injection** (listed under **Advanced** or **Developer** depending on your Squarespace version).
3. Paste the snippet into the **Header** field. The script is deferred, so it does not delay page rendering.
4. Click **Save** at the top of the panel.

The **Footer** field also works and loads the script on every page. Leave the **Lock Page** and **Order Confirmation Page** fields alone; they only apply to those single pages.
</Step>

## Troubleshooting

- **Code Injection is missing from Settings**: the site is on the Personal plan. Upgrade to Business or Commerce.
- **Only the checkout is tracked**: the snippet was pasted into **Order Confirmation Page** instead of **Header** or **Footer**.
